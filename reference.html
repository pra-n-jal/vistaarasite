The Idea

Build. Solve. Connect.

Over the course of the event, participants form teams, develop original mobile apps and web products, receive mentorship from experienced builders, and compete before a panel of industry professionals.

What Sets Vistaar Apart

Industry interaction, built into the hackathon itself

Startup founders, engineers and technology professionals conduct seminars and small-group conversations throughout the event, sharing how they identified real problems, built products around them, dealt with failure, and turned technical ideas into practical solutions.

The objective isn't to turn Vistaar into a conference. The industry component exists to make the hackathon itself better — giving students exposure to real applications and letting them bring that perspective back into what they build.

The Principle

Students shouldn't just learn how to build technology — they should understand how it's used to solve real problems.
